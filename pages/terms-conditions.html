1. Bookings

A booking is confirmed only after full payment and issue of a Booking ID. Guests must be 18+ to book. Valid government photo ID is required at check-in for all adults.

2. Check-in and Check-out

Check-in from 1:00 PM; check-out until 11:00 AM. Late check-out after 1:00 PM is charged at 50% of the nightly tariff.

3. Cancellation and Refunds

  • 7+ days before arrival: full refund
  • 3 to 7 days before arrival: 50% refund
  • Within 72 hours or no-show: no refund

Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

4. Children and Extra Beds

Children below 5 stay free without an extra bed. Children 5 to 12 are charged for activities and meals. Extra beds are Rs. 1,200 per night.

5. Activities and Safety

All adventure activities involve some risk. Guests must follow guide instructions and safety rules. Activities may be cancelled due to weather; in that case the activity fee is refunded.

6. Conduct

Smoking is permitted only in designated areas. Guests are responsible for any damage to resort property. The resort may refuse service to anyone behaving unsafely.

7. Pricing

All prices are in Indian Rupees and exclude 12% GST unless stated. Coupon codes cannot be combined.

8. Jurisdiction

These terms are governed by the laws of India; disputes are subject to the courts of Jaisalmer, Rajasthan.
