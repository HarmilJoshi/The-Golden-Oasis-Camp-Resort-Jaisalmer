Desert Safari

Hold on tight as our expert drivers take you dune-bashing across the Sam sand dunes in open 4x4 jeeps, then slow down with a traditional camel ride to the sunset point. Visit a Bishnoi village, spot chinkara gazelles and end with chai on the dunes.

Highlights

  • Open 4x4 jeep dune-bashing
  • 45-minute camel ride to sunset point
  • Visit to a traditional desert village
  • Wildlife spotting near Desert National Park
  • Masala chai and snacks on the dunes

What the Experience Looks Like

  • 4:00 PM Pick-up from resort reception
  • 4:15 PM Jeep safari across the dunes
  • 5:15 PM Village visit and camel ride
  • 6:15 PM Sunset, chai and photos
  • 7:00 PM Return for the cultural evening

Quick Info

  • Price Rs. 1,500 /person
  • Duration 3 hours
  • Age 5+ years
  • Group size Max 12
  • Timings 6:30 AM - 5:00 PM
