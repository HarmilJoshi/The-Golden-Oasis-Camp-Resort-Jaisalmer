Jungle Tent

Nestled among khejri and date-palm trees at the edge of the oasis, our Jungle Tents offer true glamping: canvas walls, rustic wooden furniture, comfortable beds and an attached bathroom. Fall asleep to the desert breeze and wake to birdsong.

Tents are steps away from the campfire arena, making them a favourite with young travellers and adventure lovers.

Amenities

  • Double bed with rollaway on request
  • Canvas walls with wooden sit-out
  • Attached bathroom
  • Air cooler and pedestal fan
  • Solar lighting
  • Torch and mosquito net
  • Charging point
  • Daily housekeeping
  • Steps from the campfire arena
  • WiFi in the lobby

House Rules

  • Check-in 1:00 PM, check-out 11:00 AM
  • Valid government photo ID required for all adults
  • Children below 5 years stay free (no extra bed)
  • Free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival

Room Details

  • Price Rs. 5,500 /night
  • Max guests 3 (with rollaway)
  • Room size 300 sq.ft
  • Bed Double + Rollaway
  • View Oasis grove
  • Meals Breakfast included
