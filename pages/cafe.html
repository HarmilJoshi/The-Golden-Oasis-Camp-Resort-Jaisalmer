Sunset Cafe

Coffee, chai and desert sunsets.

Home › Dining › Sunset Cafe

Sip as the Dunes Turn Gold

Perched on the highest point of the resort, the Sunset Cafe is our guests' favourite spot for evening chai, freshly brewed coffee, mocktails and Rajasthani snacks like pyaaz kachori and mirchi vada. Open 8 AM to 9 PM.

  • Freshly ground Arabica and filter coffee
  • Kulhad chai brewed with desert herbs
  • Bakery: croissants, cookies and cakes
  • WiFi and board games
