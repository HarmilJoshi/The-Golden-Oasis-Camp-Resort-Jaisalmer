Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you book, register, subscribe or contact us we collect your name, email address, mobile number, stay dates and preferences. For payments we keep only the payment method and a transaction reference.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm bookings
  • To send booking updates and, if you subscribe, offers
  • To improve our website and services
  • To meet legal requirements

3. Cookies

We use cookies to remember your cookie choice, your account, your login, your remembered email, your newsletter subscription and your bookings. You can delete cookies at any time from your browser settings.

Cookie Purpose Duration
cookieConsent Stores your cookie choice 1 year
user_... Your registered account 1 year
rememberEmail Remember login email 30 days
newsletter Newsletter status 1 year
oasisBookings Your bookings list 1 year

4. Sessions

We ask for your permission to use cookies once, when you login or register. Your login and the booking you are currently making are kept in session cookies. Session cookies have no expiry date, so the browser deletes them automatically when it is closed.

5. Data Sharing

We never sell your data. Information is shared only with service partners needed to deliver your stay (for example transfer drivers) or when required by law.

6. Your Rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data by writing to privacy@thegoldenoasis.in .
