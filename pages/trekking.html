Dune Trekking

Experience the silence of the Thar on foot. Our naturalist guides lead small groups across the dunes before sunrise, sharing stories of desert life, tracking footprints of foxes and beetles and stopping at a high dune to watch the sun rise.

Highlights

  • 5 km guided walk on soft dunes
  • Sunrise from the highest dune
  • Desert flora and fauna interpretation
  • Packed breakfast and water
  • Photography stops

What the Experience Looks Like

  • 6:00 AM Meet at the campfire arena
  • 6:15 AM Trek begins
  • 6:50 AM Sunrise point
  • 7:30 AM Breakfast on the dunes
  • 8:30 AM Return to resort

Quick Info

  • Price Rs. 900 /person
  • Duration 2.5 hours
  • Distance 5 km
  • Difficulty Easy to moderate
  • Age 8+ years
  • Timings 5:30 AM - 7:30 AM
