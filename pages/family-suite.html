Family Suite

Designed for families, the Family Suite features two connected bedrooms, one with a king bed and one with two single beds, plus a shared living area with a sofa-cum-bed. Kids love the board games corner, while parents appreciate the extra space and two bathrooms.

Suites are located closest to the Kids Zone and the restaurant.

Amenities

  • Two connected bedrooms
  • One king-size bed and two single beds
  • Living area with sofa-cum-bed
  • Two en-suite bathrooms
  • Two TVs
  • Board games corner
  • Baby cot on request
  • Kids welcome kit
  • Air conditioning, ceiling fans
  • Free WiFi

House Rules

  • Check-in 1:00 PM, check-out 11:00 AM
  • Valid government photo ID required for all adults
  • Children below 5 years stay free (no extra bed)
  • Free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival

Room Details

  • Price Rs. 15,000 /night
  • Max guests 5 (4 adults + 1 child)
  • Room size 820 sq.ft
  • Bed 1 King + 2 Single
  • View Garden
  • Meals Breakfast included
