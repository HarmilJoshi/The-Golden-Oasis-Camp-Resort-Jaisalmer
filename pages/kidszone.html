Kids Zone

Our supervised Kids Zone gives children (3 to 12 years) a safe space to play, create and explore while parents relax. Trained caretakers run daily activities: puppet making, pottery, treasure hunts in the sand, camel-cart rides and storytelling.

Highlights

  • Trained and police-verified caretakers
  • Indoor play room and outdoor play area
  • Arts, crafts and pottery
  • Sand treasure hunts
  • Healthy snacks included

What the Experience Looks Like

  • 9:00 AM Check-in and free play
  • 10:30 AM Arts and crafts
  • 12:30 PM Lunch and rest
  • 3:00 PM Outdoor games and treasure hunt
  • 5:00 PM Storytelling and pick-up

Quick Info

  • Price Rs. 400 /person
  • Age 3 - 12 years
  • Ratio 1 caretaker : 5 kids
  • Day pass Per child
  • Timings 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM
