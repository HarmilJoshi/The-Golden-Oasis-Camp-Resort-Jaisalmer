2011 Established

Our Story

The Golden Oasis Camp & Resort began as a cluster of six Swiss tents run by the Bhati family, who have lived by the Sam dunes for generations. Today it is a 48-key desert resort that still feels like a family home. Every guest is welcomed with a tilak, a garland and a glass of chilled kesar sharbat.

We work closely with local Manganiyar musicians, Kalbeliya dancers, camel owners and artisans, so every stay directly supports the desert community.

  • Rated 4.8 by over 25,000 guests
  • Solar-powered lighting and water recycling
  • 100% local staff from Sam and Jaisalmer villages
Our Values

Mission, Vision & Promise

Our Mission

To share the soul of the Thar desert through authentic, comfortable and sustainable hospitality.

Our Vision

To be Rajasthan's most loved desert resort, known for warmth, culture and care for the land.

Our Promise

Clean rooms, honest prices, safe adventures and a smile at every step of your journey.

Milestones

Our Journey

  • 2011 Opened with 6 Swiss tents and a campfire stage.
  • 2014 Added Deluxe Rooms and the open-air Dhola Maru restaurant.
  • 2018 Launched Luxury Cottages and the courtyard pool.
  • 2021 Went solar and started the community artisan programme.
  • 2025 Family Suites, Kids Zone and the Sunset Cafe opened.
