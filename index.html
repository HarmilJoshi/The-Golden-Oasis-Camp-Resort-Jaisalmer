Welcome to The Golden Oasis

An Authentic Desert Escape, Crafted With Care

Set on 12 acres beside the famous Sam Sand Dunes, The Golden Oasis Camp & Resort blends the timeless spirit of Rajasthani hospitality with modern comfort. Wake to peacock calls, ride camels into the sunset and dine to the music of Manganiyar folk artists.

  • Luxury cottages, suites and Swiss tents with private sit-outs
  • Multi-cuisine restaurant and Sunset Cafe
  • Jeep safari, camel rides, cultural nights and stargazing
  • Just 42 km from Jaisalmer Fort
